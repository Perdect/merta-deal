## Data safety form (what the website actually collects)
- **Personal info:** name, phone number, email (optional), delivery addresses — for account and delivery.
- **Location:** approximate and precise, only when the customer taps "Use my current location" — to check delivery coverage and fill the address.
- **Financial info:** payments are processed by Razorpay; card/UPI details are not stored by Merta Deal. Order history is stored.
- **App activity:** orders, cart, wishlist, support messages, reviews.
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Customers can ask for account deletion through Help & support.
- Not sold, not used for third-party advertising.
